# System Overview

## Purpose

HYNOVA Flow is designed to centralize the operational
workflow of a freelance or solo service business.

The system connects the major stages of the business lifecycle
into one structured environment.

---

## Business Lifecycle

### 1. Lead

Potential clients enter the system through the Leads table.

Information includes:

- Lead information
- Service interest
- Lead stage
- Priority
- Follow-up date

↓

### 2. Client

Qualified leads can become clients.

Client information is maintained centrally to avoid
duplicating customer information across the system.

↓

### 3. Project

Clients can have one or more projects.

Projects track:

- Project status
- Dates
- Client
- Revenue
- Tasks
- Progress

↓

### 4. Tasks

Projects are broken into individual tasks.

Tasks provide visibility into:

- What needs to be done
- Who is responsible
- Priority
- Due date
- Status

↓

### 5. Time

Time spent on projects can be tracked and used for
billable calculations and reporting.

↓

### 6. Invoices

Project revenue can be tracked through invoices.

Invoices contain:

- Invoice information
- Client
- Project
- Amount
- Status
- Due date

↓

### 7. Payments

Payments provide visibility into collected revenue
and outstanding amounts.

↓

### 8. Expenses

Business expenses are tracked separately and categorized
for reporting.

↓

### 9. Dashboard

Operational and financial information is aggregated
into the dashboard.

This allows the user to monitor:

- Revenue
- Expenses
- Profit
- Projects
- Follow-ups
- Operational attention items
