# Workflow

## Lead-to-Payment Workflow

HYNOVA Flow follows a simplified business lifecycle.

```text
New Lead
   ↓
Qualification
   ↓
Follow-up
   ↓
Client
   ↓
Project
   ↓
Tasks
   ↓
Time Tracking
   ↓
Invoice
   ↓
Payment
   ↓
Financial Reporting
```
## Lead Management

A lead enters the system with relevant information.

The system tracks:

- Stage
- Priority
- Follow-up date
- Contact information
- Service interest

Follow-up status helps identify leads requiring attention.

---

## Project Workflow

Once a lead becomes a client and work begins, a project can be created.

Projects act as the central connection between:

- Client
- Tasks
- Time
- Revenue

---

## Financial Workflow

Project revenue flows into invoice tracking.

Payments are recorded against invoices.

Expenses are tracked separately.

The dashboard combines these financial records to provide operational visibility.
---

## Automation

VBA is used to automate repetitive workbook operations, including system maintenance and refresh-related processes.

The automation layer is designed to reduce manual work and maintain consistency across the workbook.

---

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides a high-level operational view of the system.

It brings together:

- Revenue
- Expenses
- Profit
- Project information
- Follow-up information
- Operational alerts
